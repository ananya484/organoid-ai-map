OrganoidAI Map

Every primary study (2018–2026) that applied machine learning to organoids or organs-on-chips, from a systematic Europe PMC search, coded for task, application, validation and comparison with in vivo outcomes. Codes are from abstracts and may contain errors; corrections welcome.

Cite: Sharma A (2026) A review for almost every study: a systematic map of artificial intelligence applied to organoids and organs-on-chips, and the evidence a regulator would need. Preprint. Database: doi:10.5281/zenodo.23778313
